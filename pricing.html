Questions

How much is a class at Space Pilates in Sanford?

A single drop-in class is $29. A 10 class pack is $230, which works out to $23 per class. New members can take unlimited classes for 2 weeks for $69.

What is the best option for a new member?

The Two Week Intro Offer: unlimited classes across all four formats (Space Pilates, Space Sculpt, Space Strength and Space Yoga) for $69, so you can find your favorites before choosing a membership.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Memberships are month to month. The intro offer is a one-time purchase with no commitment.

Which membership should I choose?

Reset ($89, 4 classes a month) suits once a week. Routine ($149, 8 classes a month) suits twice a week. Lifestyle ($199, unlimited) is the best value from about three classes a week.
