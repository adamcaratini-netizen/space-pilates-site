Your First Visit

Never done Pilates? Good.

See the Schedule

That is most of the people who walk in. Wear something you can move in. Arrive ten minutes early. Mats and everything else are here. Grippy socks help but are not required, and we have them if you want a pair.

Tell whoever greets you that it is your first time. You will get a short walk-through, and the instructor will know to keep an eye on you.

You can stop, rest, or modify at any point in any class, and nobody will say a word about it. That is not a policy. That is the whole point.
