From Heathrow, Downtown Sanford is a straight run east on SR 46. Space Pilates + Wellness Society is a small mat Pilates and strength studio on East 2nd Street, about 20 minutes from the Heathrow gates.

Why Heathrow members choose Space

  • Small, coached classes, capped at 15, with modifications cued for every level.
  • Strength that lasts: Space Strength is programmed for progression by co-founder Adam Caratini, who has coached strength and conditioning for 14 years.
  • Recovery built in: restorative Yoga sits alongside mat Pilates and Sculpt in one membership.
  • A reason to go downtown: coffee, brunch and the riverfront are a short walk from the studio.

Getting here from Heathrow

Take Heathrow Boulevard to International Parkway, then east on SR 46 straight into Downtown Sanford. It is about 9 miles, 20 to 25 minutes depending on traffic. The studio is at 307 E 2nd St, Sanford, FL 32771, with street parking and public lots a short walk away in the historic district.

Weekend mornings in Sanford

Saturday and Sunday morning classes are the easy way in from Heathrow: class, then the Saturday farmers market, a coffee downtown and the RiverWalk. On weekdays, classes run from 6:00 AM to about 6:30 PM. Read a Saturday morning in Downtown Sanford for the full route.

Questions

How far is Space Pilates from Heathrow?

About 9 miles. Take International Parkway north to SR 46 and head east into Downtown Sanford: around 20 minutes without traffic.

Do I need Pilates experience?

No. Most people who walk in have never done Pilates. Tell your instructor it is your first class and they will cue options throughout.

Are there weekend classes?

Yes, on Saturday and Sunday mornings. See the live schedule for this week's times.

What does it cost?

New members get 2 weeks unlimited for $69. After that, drop-ins are $29 and memberships start at $89 a month, month to month.
