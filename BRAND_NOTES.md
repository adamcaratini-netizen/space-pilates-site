## The Four Formats
| Format | Tagline | Description |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| CLASSIC | Where Strength Begins | Mat Pilates. Foundational strength, breath, center, precision. Classical repertoire + light props (rings, bands, light weights). Good for beginners and anyone who wants the foundation done properly. |
| SCULPT | Strength, Set to a Beat | Rhythm-driven strength. Same tools as Form (dumbbells, bands, bodyweight) but set to music, faster paced, more continuous. Taught by Carey. |
| FORM | Strength, Programmed Properly | Pilates-informed strength with dumbbells, rings, bands, bodyweight. Slower, heavier than Sculpt. Built to make members durable. Programmed by Adam (14 years in S&C). |
| FLOW | Yoga, Built to Restore | Restorative yoga. Mobility, breath, and release. Not a challenge — a restoration. Draws from restorative and gentle vinyasa. |

## CRITICAL LANGUAGE RULES (from manual)
- Never frame around how members look — always what they can do / carry / feel
- No leaderboard, no performance pressure
- "Longer and stronger" — always a good phrase
- Core philosophy: "SPACE is the pause between everything else."
- Mission: "unhurried, breath-led programming"
- Never: "burn," "crush," "hustle," "earn your body," "sweat it out"

## Tone & Voice
- Purposeful, calm, confident. Not hype-y.
- Phrases that fit: "come into your space," "longer and stronger," "come back into alignment," "capacity, not performance," "nothing to prove"

## Weekly Schedule (from manual)
- Monday: Classic — Foundational Strength & Control
- Tuesday: Sculpt — To the Beat
- Wednesday: Form — Strength & Resistance
- (Flow fills recovery/restoration days)

## Class Lengths
- Classic: 45–50 min
- Sculpt: 45–55 min
- Form: 45–50 min
- Flow: 45–60 min
