The Idea

SPACE is the pause
between everything else.

In Pilates, finding space means lengthening the spine, decompressing the joints, and finding mental clarity. Movement here is not about changing yourself. It is about coming back into alignment with yourself.

Your First Visit

Never done
Pilates? Good.

That is most of the people who walk in. Wear something you can move in. Arrive ten minutes early. Tell whoever greets you it is your first time — you will get a walk-through and the instructor will keep an eye on you. You can stop, rest, or modify at any point, and nobody will say a word about it.
