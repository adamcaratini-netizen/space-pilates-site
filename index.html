In Pilates, finding space means lengthening the spine, decompressing the joints, and finding mental clarity. It is where you return to your body, your breath, and your awareness - free from pressure, noise, and performance.

We built a studio around that idea. A room designed to give back more than it asks for. Movement here is not about changing yourself. It is about coming back into alignment with yourself.

Never done Pilates?

That is most of the people who walk in. You do not need experience, a certain kind of body, or any idea what you are doing. You just need to show up.

  • Wear something you can move in
  • Arrive ten minutes early for your first class
  • Bring your water bottle and a sweat towel if you'd like, everything else is provided
  • Grippy socks required - we have them for sale if needed
  • Tell us it is your first time - we will walk you through it
  • Modify or rest at any point, any class, any time
