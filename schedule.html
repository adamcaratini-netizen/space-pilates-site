Questions

How do I book a class at Space Pilates?

Tap Book next to any class to reserve it in the Space booking system, or use the Space Pilates app on iPhone or Android. New members can start with 2 weeks of unlimited classes for $69.

What happens if a class is full?

Tap Join waitlist. If a spot opens, the booking system moves you in and lets you know.

How early should I arrive for my first class?

About 10 minutes early, so the instructor can meet you and set you up.
