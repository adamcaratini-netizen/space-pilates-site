The Classes

Three formats.
One room.

Mat, board, and strength — each one complete on its own, better together. Every class is fifteen people. Every instructor knows your name.

01
GROUND
Classical Mat

The original practice, on the floor, with nothing between you and the work. Classical mat repertoire with small props — rings, bands, weights — built to lengthen the spine, decompress the joints, and teach the body to move from its center. No equipment to learn. No machine to be intimidated by. Just the floor, your breath, and fifty minutes of undivided attention.

02
GLIDE
Heroboard

Reformer-style resistance, on a platform that moves under you. GLIDE uses the Heroboard — a sliding platform that recreates the resistance-and-control of reformer work. The carriage moves, your body stabilizes, and everything gets harder than it looks. This is the class that builds control you can feel in the rest of your life. It is not a reformer, and we will never tell you it is. It is its own thing, and it is very good at what it does.

03
FORM
Strength

Pilates-informed strength work — dumbbells, rings, bands, and bodyweight, sequenced by a coach with fourteen years in strength and conditioning. Slower than a bootcamp. Heavier than a mat class. Built to make you durable. Strength is not the opposite of ease. It is what makes ease possible. A body that can carry more moves through the world with less strain.

50 min
