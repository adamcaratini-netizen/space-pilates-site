A Saturday Morning in Downtown Sanford: Pilates, Coffee and the RiverWalk

How to spend a Saturday morning in Historic Downtown Sanford, FL: an early Pilates or strength class on 2nd Street, coffee, the farmers market and a walk on the Lake Monroe RiverWalk.

By Carey Caratini, founder of Space Pilates + Wellness Society. Pilates instructor, NASM certified personal trainer and mental health therapist practicing as a Registered Mental Health Counselor Intern in Florida.

A good Saturday morning in Downtown Sanford starts early and stays on foot. Take a class at Space on East 2nd Street, walk a couple of blocks for coffee, browse the farmers market on 1st Street, then finish with an easy walk along Lake Monroe on the RiverWalk. Everything in this guide is within a short walk of the studio, so you can park once and leave the car for the morning.

Key takeaways

  • Space runs Saturday morning classes, usually starting at 8 and 9 o'clock. Check the live class schedule for this week's times.
  • Coffee, a sit-down breakfast and the Saturday farmers market are all a few minutes' walk from the studio.
  • The Sanford RiverWalk runs along Lake Monroe and is the easiest way to cool down after class.
  • New to Space? The 2 week intro gives you unlimited classes for $69, so a Saturday habit costs very little to try.

Why start the weekend with a class

Saturday is the one morning many people have without an alarm, a commute or a school run. That makes it a good time to move with some attention instead of squeezing a workout between obligations. A class gives the morning a shape: you show up at a set time, someone else plans the session, and for 45 minutes the only job is to follow your breath and the cues.

It also changes how the rest of the day feels. People often describe leaving a Pilates or strength class feeling taller, looser and more awake, and in a better mood to spend the day out. You do not need to be fit already, and you do not need to have done Pilates before. Every class at Space is built for mixed levels, with options to make each exercise easier or harder.

8:00 or 9:00: class at Space

Space Pilates + Wellness Society is at 307 E 2nd St, in the historic district. Saturday classes are small, with a maximum of 15 people, so the instructor can see everyone and adjust what you are doing.

Which class to choose depends on how you want to feel afterward:

  • Space Strength is slower, heavier strength training with dumbbells, kettlebells and bands. Choose it if you want to feel strong and steady.
  • Space Sculpt is Pilates-inspired strength set to music, faster and continuous. Choose it if you want energy and a good playlist.
  • Space Pilates is classical mat Pilates: breath, deep core, control and alignment. Choose it if you are new, or if your back and hips want some care.
  • Space Yoga is restorative yoga for mobility, breath and slowing down. Choose it if the week was long.

If it is your first class, arrive about 10 minutes early so the instructor can meet you. Wear fitted clothes you can move in and bring grippy socks if you have them. Our guide on what to wear to Pilates covers the details, and Pilates for beginners explains what the first class feels like.

After class: coffee a couple of blocks away

You will want something warm or cold to drink, and Downtown Sanford has independent coffee within a few minutes' walk of the studio. Palate Coffee Brewery is a short walk west along 2nd Street, and there are more cafes on and around 1st Street. Hours change, so check before you go, especially on Sundays.

If you would rather sit down for breakfast, the Colonial Room on East 1st Street is a long-standing downtown diner for breakfast and lunch. Weekend mornings can be busy, which is one more reason to take the early class and get there first.

Mid-morning: the farmers market on 1st Street

On Saturdays a farmers market sets up downtown, around East 1st Street and Magnolia Avenue, with produce, baked goods, plants and local makers. It is a relaxed way to spend half an hour, and it suits the post-class mood: no rush, plenty to look at, and something good to take home for lunch. Check the market's current hours on its page before you plan around it.

Late morning: a walk on the RiverWalk

The Sanford RiverWalk follows the shore of Lake Monroe for several miles, wide and flat, with benches, shade in places and the lake on one side. From the studio, head north a few blocks to the water. You do not need to walk far. Ten or fifteen minutes out and back is enough to let your legs settle after a strength class and to finish the morning outside.

If you have kids or a dog with you, Fort Mellon Park sits along the RiverWalk and is an easy place to stop.

Make it a habit

One good Saturday is pleasant. A Saturday class every week is where the real changes come from. Pilates and strength work build gradually, and most people notice the difference in posture, strength and how they move after a few weeks of steady practice. Our guide on how often to do Pilates explains how to build a routine that fits your week.

A simple plan is to keep Saturday as your anchor class, then add one weekday class when you can. The Reset membership covers four classes a month, which is one Saturday class every week. If you want to try more first, the 2 week intro lets you take as many classes as you like across all four formats.

Questions

Is there parking near Space Pilates in Downtown Sanford?

Yes. Downtown Sanford has street parking and public lots within a short walk of 307 E 2nd St. Give yourself a few extra minutes on busy weekend mornings.

What time are Saturday classes at Space?

Saturday classes usually start in the morning, often at 8 and 9 o'clock, with some weeks adding a later class. Times change week to week, so check the live class schedule before you go.

Can I come to a Saturday class if I have never done Pilates?

Yes. Every class is built for mixed levels, and instructors cue easier and harder options throughout. Tell the instructor it is your first class and arrive about 10 minutes early.

How long is the walk from the studio to the RiverWalk?

It is a few blocks north of the studio to Lake Monroe, a few minutes on foot. From there you can walk as far along the water as you like.
