Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Do I need experience to join?

No. Most people who walk in for the first time have never done Pilates before. Every class is accessible - instructors cue modifications throughout. Just tell us it is your first time.

What is included in the 2-week intro?

Unlimited access to all four class formats - Pilates, Sculpt, Strength, and Yoga - for 14 days. Take as many classes as you want across our full weekly schedule.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, a water bottle, and a sweat towel. Grippy socks are required and available for purchase at the front desk.

What are the four class formats?

Space Pilates is classical mat Pilates - the foundation. Space Sculpt is rhythm-driven strength set to music. Space Strength is slower, heavier strength work programmed for lasting results. Space Yoga is restorative yoga focused on mobility and breath.

What happens after the 2 weeks?

Someone from our team will reach out to walk you through membership options. No pressure, no hard sell. We will find what fits your schedule.
