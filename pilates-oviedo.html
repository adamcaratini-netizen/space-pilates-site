Most Pilates studios in Oviedo are reformer-based. If you want classical mat Pilates, strength training and restorative yoga under one membership, Space Pilates + Wellness Society is about 20 minutes north on SR 417 in Historic Downtown Sanford.

What Oviedo members get at Space

  • Mat Pilates done properly. Space Pilates is the classical mat repertoire with light props. Many of those exercises are harder on the mat because no springs assist you. See mat vs reformer Pilates.
  • Strength and yoga in the same membership: Space Strength, Space Sculpt and Space Yoga.
  • Small classes, capped at 15, led by certified instructors.
  • Month to month: no long-term contract, and drop-ins are $29.

Getting here from Oviedo

Take SR 434 or SR 419 to the SR 417 toll road, then north to Sanford. It is about 12 miles, 20 to 25 minutes depending on traffic. The studio is at 307 E 2nd St, Sanford, FL 32771, with street parking and public lots a short walk away in the historic district.

Making the drive worth it

From Oviedo, a realistic week is two or three classes rather than every day: a weekday class at 6:00 AM or after work, plus a Saturday or Sunday morning class with time downtown after. The Routine membership (8 classes a month) fits that pattern.

Questions

How far is Space Pilates from Oviedo?

About 12 miles. From central Oviedo, take SR 434 or SR 419 to the SR 417 toll road and head north to Sanford: around 20 minutes without traffic.

Is Space a reformer studio?

No. Space is a mat studio. Space Pilates is classical mat Pilates with light props; there are no reformer machines.

Which membership suits someone driving from Oviedo?

Routine ($149 a month, 8 classes) suits two classes a week. Start with the 2 week intro for $69 to find the formats and times that work for you.

What does it cost?

New members get 2 weeks unlimited for $69. After that, drop-ins are $29 and memberships start at $89 a month, month to month.
