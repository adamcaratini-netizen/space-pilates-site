The Studio.

Our Story

SPACE is the pause between everything else.

In Pilates, finding space means lengthening the spine, decompressing the joints, and finding mental clarity. It is where you return to your body, your breath, and your awareness — free from pressure, noise, and performance.

We built a studio around that idea. A room designed to give back more than it asks for. Movement here is not about changing yourself. It is about coming back into alignment with yourself.

Our mission is simple: to build strength, control, and mobility through unhurried, breath-led programming — and to give members back more room than they walked in with.

Movement here is not about changing yourself. It is about coming back into alignment with yourself.

The SPACE Method.

Every class at SPACE is built around the same set of principles. Speed is not the goal. Fatigue is not the goal. Consistency beats intensity — every time.

We coach the center first. Then control. Then everything else follows. No leaderboard, no performance pressure. Fifteen people, one room, one intention.

Four formats, one method. Each one was designed to complement the others — so whether you come once a week or five times, the work compounds.

01  —  Mat Pilates

Space CLASSIC

Where Strength Begins

Classical mat Pilates built on breath, center, and precision. The full classical repertoire with light props — rings, bands, light weights. Master the foundation, and everything else gets easier. Good for beginners and anyone who wants the foundation done properly.

45–50 min  ·  All levels

02  —  Strength & Rhythm

Space SCULPT

Strength, Set to a Beat

The same Pilates-informed work as Form — dumbbells, bands, bodyweight — moved to music. Faster, more continuous, and built to prove that precision can keep up with the rhythm.

45–55 min  ·  Beginner to advanced

03  —  Strength

Space FORM

Strength, Programmed Properly

Pilates-informed strength with dumbbells, rings, and bands. Slower, heavier, and built to last. Strength is not the opposite of ease — it is what makes ease possible.

45–50 min  ·  All levels

04  —  Restore

Space FLOW

Yoga, Built to Restore

Where Classic, Sculpt, and Form build capacity, Flow gives it back — mobility, breath, and release given the room they don't get anywhere else in the week. Nothing to prove. Just space to land.

45–60 min  ·  All levels

The people BEHIND IT.

Carey Caratini

Co-Founder  ·  Pilates Instructor  ·  Licensed Therapist

Carey is a certified Pilates instructor and a licensed mental health counselor with a master's in Clinical Mental Health Counseling from UCF. The way she teaches comes from a real, clinical understanding of the nervous system — not just how a body looks in a mirror.

She and Adam spent a long time watching people push themselves in rooms that gave nothing back. SPACE is the other kind of room — built around breath, intention, and the belief that every person who walks in belongs exactly where they are.

  • NASM Certified Personal Trainer
  • Certified Pilates Instructor
  • Licensed Therapist (LMHC) — Clinical Mental Health Counseling, UCF

Adam Caratini

Co-Founder  ·  Strength Coach

Adam has spent fourteen years as a strength coach — building and running two gyms, programming for athletes and everyday people, and learning what actually works when the goal is longevity, not just performance.

He programs the strength side of every SPACE format the way strength should be programmed: slowly, deliberately, and with a reason for every set.

  • 14 years as a strength and conditioning coach
